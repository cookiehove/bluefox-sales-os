Numbered routes

The unvisited sites are split into numbered routes, each an area one salesman can finish in a day. Press Claim or Assign on a route to give it to a salesman. It becomes his checklist, and everyone on the team sees whose it is.

Splitting again redraws every route from the unvisited sites showing on the map, starting and ending at home base, with the fewest total miles. It uses the hours and minutes per stop set under Plan a single day below. Routes already claimed keep their stops.

Plan a single day

Click any site on the map and choose Add to route, or use the buttons. The stops are put in the shortest driving order, starting and ending at home base.

    Claimed routes
