Daily routes

Plan a route

Starts and ends at home base, Weddington. Picks job sites nobody has visited yet and skips anything already on the other salesman's route that day.

Drive times are estimates from map distance, not live traffic. Open the stops in Google Maps for real directions.

Numbered routes

Splits every unvisited job site into numbered routes, each an area one salesman can finish in a day, starting and ending at home base, choosing the split with the fewest total miles. It uses the hours, minutes per stop and site types chosen above. Everyone on the team sees the same numbered routes.

Job sites

Job sites pulled November 22, 2026. General contractor offices added November 23, 2026 as a starter list, not every contractor: addresses come from North Carolina contractor licence records, the Carolinas AGC member directory and company websites, and each office says which. Mecklenburg County: open building permits issued since March 1, 2026. Cabarrus County: open zoning permits for new homes and commercial work since January 2025. Lincoln County: open new-home permits since January 2025. Union County, Monroe, Waxhaw, Indian Trail, Kannapolis, Kings Mountain and Kershaw County SC: projects their planning offices list as under construction. Commercial new builds and additions are 1,500 sq ft or larger. Commercial remodels and upfits are kept at $250,000 or more in stated cost. New homes are kept only where the permit shows $600,000 or more (lowered from $700,000 on November 26, 2026) in stated construction cost; for a community of homes, that is the average per home. Outside Mecklenburg County the records carry no value, so those homes are judged by the typical home value of their area (Zillow, October 2026) plus 10% for new construction, and kept only at $700,000 or more (this part was not re-run when the line moved to $600,000). Today that means Weddington, Waxhaw, Wesley Chapel and Davidson. Home remodels and additions are left off. New homes close together are grouped into one community stop. Mass-built neighborhoods (one builder putting up many homes) were added November 25, 2026 as one stop each, at the model home or sales center where the builder's staff sit: every such neighborhood found in Mecklenburg and Union County records was ranked by home price, the cheaper half was left off, and the single-home price rule does not apply to them. On November 26, 2026 that list was cut again to the ones still being built and in the earlier stages: homes under construction now, and no more than half of the lots already sold to homeowners (where the county has no lot records yet, a home started within the last 5 months instead). Each stop shows its build stage. Later that day the list was trimmed at the owner's direction: builder neighborhoods were cut to the ones priced around $600,000 and up; remodels inside office suites, tower floors and restricted sites (airport, hospitals, data centers, schools, government) were removed. Jobs estimated to be past their finish are still listed and say so. Finish dates are estimates from the permit date and the size of the job, not site visits. Each one says whether its model home was confirmed by a county permit, taken from the builder's published address, is only a likely match from tax records, or was not found. York, Lancaster, Gaston, Iredell, Rowan, Stanly and other nearby counties are not loaded yet. Around Route 49 in Kershaw County the cut-off reaches 5 miles past the 1.75-hour ring.
